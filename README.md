# 💼 Lever Scraper

Lever Scraper is a **free and open-source** scraper that gets you **unlimited** Lever job data from 1,500+ companies for free.

## ✨ What Can I Get?

- 🔍 **Search 61,000+ live jobs at 1,500+ companies** — keyword, location, remote, country, salary & posting date
- 📄 **Full job postings** — description, requirements, benefits, salary, team & company logo
- 🏢 **Company hiring profiles** — open roles, hiring pace, jobs by team, location & workplace type
- 📝 **Application forms & new-job feeds** — every question an application asks, plus jobs posted in the last 24h or 7 days

## 📚 Endpoints

8 endpoints cover everything you need.

| Endpoint | Path | Returns |
|---|---|---|
| Search Jobs | `/jobs/search` | Live jobs across 1,500+ companies, filtered your way |
| Job Details | `/jobs/details` | One full posting: description, requirements, salary, company logo |
| New Jobs | `/jobs/new` | Jobs posted in the last 24h, 7 days or since any date |
| Company Jobs | `/companies/jobs` | Every open job at one company, with filters |
| Company Details | `/companies/details` | Hiring profile: open roles, hiring pace, jobs by team and location |
| Job Application Form | `/jobs/application-form` | Every question an application asks, custom questions included |
| Find Company Board | `/companies/resolve` | A company's Lever board from its name or website |
| Search Companies | `/companies/search` | 1,500+ companies hiring on Lever, by country and size |
